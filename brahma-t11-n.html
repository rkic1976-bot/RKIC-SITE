The Brahma T11/N is an induction ignition transformer for gas and oil burners, stepping up a 220-230V, 1.1A, 50Hz primary supply to a 9kV peak secondary voltage at 20mA RMS output current for firing a single ignition electrode. It has a 33% duty cycle over a 3-minute cycle (intermittent-spark duty, not for continuous operation, unlike the 100%-ED T13/T17 series), and operates within -10°C to 35°C ambient. The "/N" suffix denotes a non-standard mounting variant of the base T11 transformer. Manufactured by Brahma, Italy and CE marked, it weighs under 2kg and is sized to a compact 98x98x80mm footprint for burner ignition duty.
