The Brahma BE9*GMO is a replacement solenoid coil designed for the EG40*L*GMO series gas solenoid valve. Rated Class A with a maximum working pressure of 200 mbar, it runs on a 230V, 50-60Hz supply drawing 37W in normal operation and 138W during the 120-second start phase, with IP54 protection for reliable operation in industrial burner and boiler gas train installations. Manufactured by Brahma, Italy, it is RoHS compliant and CE marked, and is used as a spare coil to service or replace the coil on an existing EG40 valve without changing the valve body.
