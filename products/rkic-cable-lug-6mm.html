R.K. Instruments & Controls supplies the 6.0mm Female Bullet/Spade Cable Lug, a non-insulated female quick-disconnect crimp terminal engineered for high-voltage ignition circuits such as ignition transformer connectors and ceramic ignition pin terminations. Stamped from 0.5mm phosphor bronze — offering greater elasticity, fatigue resistance and electrical conductivity than standard brass — the terminal measures 20mm in overall length with a 8.4mm back-opening / 9.4mm outer-wing mating width, a 6.0mm receptacle inner diameter and 7.3mm outer barrel diameter, and accepts conductors up to 10 mm² in cross-section. A standard ignition-circuit spare used across burner and boiler control panels wherever a 6.0mm high-voltage bullet-style termination is required.
