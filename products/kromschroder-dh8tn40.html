Honeywell Kromschröder DH8TN40 Push Button Valve

TypePush-to-Test Gauge Isolation Valve
Connection Size¼" x ¼" FNPT (Internal Thread NPT)
Body MaterialBrass, nickel plated
Maximum Inlet Pressure (pu)5 bar (70 psig)
MediaGas and air
Order No.KS03152142
ManufacturerHoneywell Kromschröder

The Honeywell Kromschröder DH8TN40 is a manual push-to-test gauge isolation valve, brass body with nickel-plated finish, sized ¼" x ¼" FNPT. It protects a pressure gauge against pressure surges by isolating it from the line; pressing the button connects the gauge for a reading, and releasing it shuts off flow to the gauge while automatically venting residual pressure. Rated to a maximum inlet pressure of 5 bar (70 psig) for gas and air. Manufactured by Honeywell Kromschröder.
