Ignition Transformers ET 401A1

Honeywell Ignition Transformer ET 401A1

Product Code: ET 401A1 · Flying leads, 3-wire
TypeET 401A1
Primary110–120V, 0.3A, 50/60 Hz
Secondary14kV Ampl., 30mA rms
Duty CycleAB 33%, ED in 3 Min.
ProtectionIP40
WiringBlue-N, Brown-L, Green&Yellow-E (flying leads)
ApprovalsUL, cUL Listed
OriginMade in China
ManufacturerHoneywell

The Honeywell ET 401A1 is an ignition transformer that steps up a 110–120V mains supply to a 14kV high-voltage secondary output rated at 30mA rms, for firing the spark electrode on gas and oil burners. Rated for an intermittent duty cycle of 33% ED (energised duration) within a 3-minute cycle, with IP40 protection, it delivers reliable high-voltage ignition for burner and boiler applications. UL/cUL listed, it terminates in flying leads (Blue-N, Brown-L, Green&Yellow-E) for direct wiring into the burner control circuit. Manufactured by Honeywell.
