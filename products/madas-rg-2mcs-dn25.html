Madas RG/2MCS DN25 Gas Pressure Regulator

Product Code: RCS040000 · DN25, 0.5-5 bar
TypeRG/2MCS DN 25
Product CodeRCS040000 · 5XX2
Inlet Pressure (PS=Pe)0.5 – 5 bar
Outlet Pressure (Pa)90 – 170 mbar
FunctionPressure regulator with relief valve, without safety shut-offs
StandardsEN 88-2, EN 13611
Type-Approval Certificate51BT3739
ComplianceCE, ATEX II 2G / II 2D, Regulation (EU) 2016/426, PED 2014/68/EU
GasNatural gas, LPG and other non-corrosive dry gases

The Madas RG/2MCS DN25 is a gas pressure regulator that supplies a preset, constant downstream pressure despite variations in inlet pressure or flow rate, using a compensated obturator for precision even under sudden inlet pressure changes. It includes a relief valve that discharges small gas flows outward if downstream overpressure occurs, but does not include OPSO/UPSO safety shut-off devices. Rated for a 0.5–5 bar inlet pressure range and ATEX zone II 2G / II 2D, it is suited to civil and industrial installations running natural gas, LPG or other non-corrosive dry gases. Manufactured by MADAS s.r.l., Verona, Italy, to EN 88-2 and EN 13611 standards.
