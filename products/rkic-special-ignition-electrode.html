Industrial Special Ignition Electrode — Threaded Spark-Plug Type Igniter

Product Code: Custom / Made to Order · M14x1.25 / M18x1.50 Thread
Product TypeIndustrial Special Ignition Electrode (Threaded Spark-Plug Type Igniter, Single Electrode)
Thread / Mounting SizeM14 x 1.25 or M18 x 1.50 — per customer specification
Overall Length (LG)Customer specified — typical range 35 mm to 600 mm
Electrode Wire Diameter (ØME)Approx. 3.5 mm — per customer specification
Electrode & Wiring MaterialKanthal A1 (FeCrAl resistance wire)
Insulator MaterialLow-loss, alkali-free Steatite Ceramic or 92–95% Aluminium Oxide
Typical ApplicationGas engines, CHP / cogeneration plants, biogas & natural gas gensets, industrial burners
CustomizationThread size, length, wire diameter and material available on demand
ManufacturerR.K. Instruments & Controls

R.K. Instruments & Controls manufactures and supplies Industrial Special Ignition Electrodes — threaded, spark-plug-style igniters used for direct spark ignition in gas engines, CHP (Combined Heat & Power) plants, biogas and natural gas generator sets, and industrial burners. Built to the widely used M14 x 1.25 or M18 x 1.50 threaded mounting format (compatible with standard threaded electrode ports), each unit is manufactured to order with a Kanthal A1 (FeCrAl) resistance-wire electrode tip, low-loss steatite or high-alumina ceramic insulation, and a customer-specified overall length. Thread size, length, wire diameter and electrode material can all be customised on request for gas engine OEMs, CHP operators and burner service applications.
