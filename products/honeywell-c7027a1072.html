Honeywell Minipeeper UV Detector C7027A1072

Product Code: C7027A1072 · Threaded mount, flying leads
TypeC7027A1072 (Minipeeper)
Sensor TypeSelf-checking UV Flame Sensor (Minipeeper)
Operating Conditions-40°C to 102°C
Lead Length2.44 m (8 ft), 18 AWG, NEC Class 1
MountingCollar, 1/2-14 NPSM internal threads, for 1/2" sight pipe
Electrical Connection1/2" spud-type connector
Max. Pressure Rating5 psi (34.5 kPa)
ApprovalsUL Listed (MP268), CSA Certified, FM Approved
ManufacturerHoneywell

The Honeywell C7027A1072 (Minipeeper) is a compact self-checking ultraviolet (UV) flame sensor used in burner management and flame safeguard systems to detect the UV radiation emitted by gas or oil flames. It mounts via a 1/2-14 NPSM threaded collar onto a 1/2" sight pipe, has a 1/2" spud-type electrical connector, and connects via a 2.44 m (8 ft) pair of flying leads to the flame safeguard control or amplifier. Rated for an operating temperature range of -40°C to 102°C and a maximum pressure of 5 psi. UL Listed, CSA Certified and FM Approved. Manufactured by Honeywell, it is a common replacement sensor for Honeywell burner flame monitoring and safety-shutdown systems.
