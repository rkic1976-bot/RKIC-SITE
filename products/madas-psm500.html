Madas PSM500 Gas Pressure Switch

Product Code: PSM500 · Range 100–500 mbar
ModelPSM500
TypeGas Pressure Switch (adjustable minimum pressure switch)
Adjustable Setting Range100 – 500 mbar
Maximum Pressure (Pmax)600 mbar (Gas)
Electrical Rating6A, 250VAC, 50/60Hz
Protection RatingIP54
ID No.CE-1015CU0757
CertificationCE 1015-19
ManufacturerMadas

The Madas PSM500 is an adjustable gas pressure switch used to monitor and signal pressure conditions in gas control lines, with a dial-set adjustable range of 100 to 500 mbar and a maximum working pressure of 600 mbar on gas service. It is rated 6A at 250VAC, 50/60Hz, carries an IP54 protection rating, and is CE certified (CE 1015-19). Manufactured by Madas.
