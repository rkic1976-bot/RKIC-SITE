Honeywell Maxon 47232 Spark Ignition Electrode

Product Code: 47232 / 47232MAXON / MX47232 / 90-25663
Part Number / MPN47232 (also 47232MAXON, MX47232, 90-25663)
Total Length5.875 in (150 mm)
Tip Diameter0.375 in (3/8") without tip extension
Thread Size1/2" NPT
Cut TypeIP-19 cut
MaterialsCeramic insulation, Kanthal alloy electrode rod
Electrode & Wiring MaterialKanthal A1 (FeCrAl resistance wire)
ManufacturerHoneywell Maxon

The Honeywell Maxon 47232 is a heavy-duty industrial spark ignitor that provides direct spark ignition for pilot-lit burner configurations in high-demand thermal processing environments. It measures 5.875 inches (150 mm) in total length with a 0.375 inch (3/8") tip diameter (without tip extension) and mounts via a 1/2" NPT thread, finished with an IP-19 cut. Built with ceramic insulation and a Kanthal alloy electrode rod for durability under high heat, it is also referenced under part numbers 47232MAXON, MX47232, and 90-25663. Manufactured by Honeywell Maxon.
