Brahma Solenoid Valve EG12*SR*GMO

Product Code: 13912001 · DN15 · 1/2" BSP
TypeEG12*SR*GMO
ConnectionDN15 · 1/2" BSP
ClassClass A
Max PressureP max. 500 mbar
Power Supply230V, 50-60Hz, 20VA
ProtectionIP54
Solenoid TypeBE6*GMO · Code 18811000

The Brahma EG12*SR*GMO is a Class A automatic gas solenoid valve for burner, boiler and gas-train shut-off duty on 1/2" (DN15) BSP connections. Rated to a higher 500 mbar max inlet pressure with IP54 enclosure protection, it runs on a 230V, 50-60Hz, 20VA coil, making it suited to applications needing extra pressure headroom over the standard EG12*L*GMO.
