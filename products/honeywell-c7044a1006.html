Honeywell UV Flame Sensor C7044A1006

Product Code: C7044A1006 · Base bracket mount, flying leads
TypeC7044A1006
Sensor TypeUV Flame Sensor, Stainless Steel Body (Minipeeper)
Flame DetectionSide or end viewing (side viewing 1/8 as sensitive as end)
Lead Length2.44 m (8 ft), 18 AWG, NEC Class 1
MountingBracket, secured by 2x 8-32 RHIS (European M-4) screws
Electrical ConnectionClamp-type, for 1/2" flexible metallic conduit
ApprovalsUL Listed (MP268), CSA Certified, FM Approved
ManufacturerHoneywell

The Honeywell C7044A1006 is a self-checking Minipeeper® ultraviolet (UV) flame sensor with a stainless-steel body, used in burner management and flame safeguard systems to detect the UV radiation emitted by gas or oil flames. Its housing has two openings permitting either side or end viewing (side viewing is 1/8 as sensitive as end viewing), making it particularly suitable for blast-tube mounting. It mounts via a bracket secured by two 8-32 RHIS (European M-4) screws, has a clamp-type connector for 1/2" flexible metallic conduit, and connects via a 2.44 m (8 ft) pair of flying leads to the flame safeguard control or amplifier. UL Listed, CSA Certified and FM Approved. Manufactured by Honeywell, it is a common replacement sensor for Honeywell burner flame monitoring and safety-shutdown systems.
