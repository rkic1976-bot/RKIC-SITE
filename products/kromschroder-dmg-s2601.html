The Honeywell Kromschröder DMG S2601 (Order No. 03352141) is a handheld electronic pressure gauge used to measure positive, negative and differential pressure of air and non-aggressive gaseous media, with a measuring range of ±150 mbar (maximum 180 mbar) and an accuracy of ±1.0% of the measured value. It features a digital LCD display with switchable pressure units and hold / min-max functions, and is powered by 2 x 1.5V batteries (LR6/AA) or a USB power adapter. The plasticized PVC housing with securing magnet carries an IP40 protection rating (EN 60529) and an ambient operating range of 0°C to +40°C. Supplied complete with a dedicated tube & connection accessory kit (Part No. 03352147) — silicone hoses (5 x 2 mm dia., 1 m, with plastic adapter) and PU tubes (2.7 x 0.65 mm dia., 0.5 m, with brass adapter), plus assorted plastic and brass connectors — suited to burner adjustment, chimney draught measurement, HVAC systems, filter/duct checks and general process pressure measurement. Manufactured by Honeywell Kromschröder (Elster GmbH, Osnabrück, Germany).
