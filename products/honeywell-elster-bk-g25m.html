The Honeywell Elster BK-G25M is a G25-size diaphragm gas meter rated for a maximum flow of 40 m³/h (minimum flow 0.25 m³/h), with a physical cyclic volume of 1.20 litres and accuracy class 1.5. It is a compact, commercial-grade meter engineered for high accuracy, long-term stability, and quiet operation, built with a stadium-shaped synthetic diaphragm. Rated to a maximum operating pressure of 0.5 bar, with an ambient/measuring temperature range of -25°C to +55°C, it carries a pulse value of 1 imp = 0.01 m³ and is EN 1359:1998 compliant. Manufactured by Honeywell Elster.
