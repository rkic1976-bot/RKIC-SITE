The Honeywell Elster BK-G10M is a G10-size diaphragm gas meter rated for a nominal flow of 10 m³/h and a maximum flow of 16 m³/h (minimum flow 0.1 m³/h), with a physical cyclic volume of 6 litres and accuracy class 1.5. It operates on a pneumatic control principle, moving a synthetic diaphragm to track gas flow while ensuring quiet operation and long-term stability, and is available in two-pipe or co-axial connection configurations with a robust folded sheet-steel outer casing. Rated to a maximum operating pressure of 0.5 bar (500 mbar), with an ambient/measuring temperature range of -25°C to +55°C and an operating gas temperature range of -25°C to +40°C, it is suitable for natural gas, LPG, and other manufactured non-corrosive gases, with a pulse value of 1 imp. = 0.01 m³. Manufactured by Honeywell Elster.
