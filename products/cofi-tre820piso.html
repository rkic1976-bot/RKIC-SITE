Ignition Transformers TRE820PISO

Cofi Ignition Transformer TRE820PISO

Product Code: TRE820PISO · Flying leads
TypeTRE820PISO
Product Code02198794
Primary230V, 1.0.8A, 50/60Hz
Secondary1 x 8kV, 20mA, Ampl. 11kV
Duty CycleED 19% in 3 Min.
MarkingCE
OriginMade in Italy
ManufacturerCofi srl

The Cofi TRE820PISO is an ignition transformer that steps up a 230V, 50/60Hz primary supply (0.8A) to a single 8kV, 20mA secondary output with an amplitude of 11kV, for firing the spark electrode on gas and oil burners. Rated for an intermittent duty cycle of 19% ED within a 3-minute cycle, it terminates in flying leads for direct wiring into the burner control circuit. Manufactured by Cofi srl, Italy, it is CE marked and suited to industrial burner and boiler ignition applications.
