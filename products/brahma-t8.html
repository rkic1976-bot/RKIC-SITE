Brahma Ignition Transformer T8

Product Code: 15000081 · T8
TypeT8
Primary230V · 2.1A · 50Hz
Secondary2 x 6500 Vrms · 33 mA
Duty CycleAB 33% ED in 3 min · T35E
GroundingCenter grounded

The Brahma T8 is a high-voltage ignition transformer that converts standard 230V mains supply into the dual 6500 Vrms secondary output needed to fire the electrode spark gap on gas and oil burners. Its center-grounded, symmetrical secondary winding drives the ignition electrode reliably at start-up, and the potted, sealed enclosure protects the internal windings in industrial burner and boiler installations. Rated for a 33% duty cycle (3-minute cycle) matching typical burner start sequences.
