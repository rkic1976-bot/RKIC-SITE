Honeywell Elster BK-G6M Diaphragm Gas Meter G6

Product Code: BK-G6M · Size and Capacity G6
ModelBK-G6M
Size and CapacityG6 · Qmax 10 m³/h

ManufacturerHoneywell Elster

The Honeywell Elster BK-G6M is a G6-size diaphragm gas meter rated for a minimum flow of 0.06 m³/h, transitional flow of 0.6 m³/h and maximum flow of 10 m³/h, with a cyclic volume of 2 litres as standard (4 litres on the variant). It uses four measuring chambers separated by synthetic, stadium-shaped diaphragms with a pneumatic control mechanism, and is available in co-axial (one-pipe) or two-pipe housing versions built in pressed steel or aluminium. The BK-G6T variant adds mechanical bimetallic temperature compensation to adjust the diaphragm stroke for the current gas temperature. Rated to a maximum operating pressure of 0.5 bar (50 kPa), with a standard temperature range of -10°C to +40°C (extended gas temperature range -25°C to +55°C), it can optionally carry a Low Frequency (LF) pulse transmitter (0.1 m³/pulse) for remote reading and is EN 1359 and MID certified by PTB. Manufactured by Honeywell Elster.
