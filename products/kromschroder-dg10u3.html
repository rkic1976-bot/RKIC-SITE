The Honeywell Kromschröder DG 10U-3 (Order No. 84447300) is a gas pressure switch used to monitor and signal pressure conditions in gas control lines, rated to a maximum working pressure of 500 mbar on gas service. It carries an SPDT switch contact rated 250 VAC, 5(1) A, an IP54 protection rating, and an ambient temperature range of -20°C to +80°C, housed with a clear protective terminal cover. Certified CE-0063CU2201 (Type S) and UKCA marked. Manufactured by Honeywell Kromschröder, Germany.
