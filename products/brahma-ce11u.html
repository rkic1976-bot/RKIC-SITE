Brahma Sequence Controller CE11U

Product Code: 37051010 · 230V, 50-60Hz, 7VA
TypeCE11U
Product Code37051010
SeriesControl Microflat
FunctionLock-out (manual reset)
Pre-Purge Time (TW)1.5 s
Safety Time (TS)10 s
Voltage230V, 50-60Hz, 7VA
Min. Flame Signal1.2 µA
Valve Outputs1 (single gas valve)
ComplianceCE 0051, PIN 63AQ0625

The Brahma CE11U is a compact burner sequence controller from the Brahma "Control Microflat" series, managing the start-up, ignition, and flame-monitoring cycle for single-valve gas burners with a 1.5-second pre-purge time and a 10-second safety time. It is a lock-out type control, requiring a manual reset via its SB button after a fault, and monitors flame current directly with a minimum detectable signal of 1.2 µA. It drives a 20kV ignition transformer via its JT1/JT2 terminals and supplies a single gas valve through its J2 terminals. Running on a 230V, 50-60Hz, 7VA supply, it is manufactured by Brahma, Italy, for automatic burner control on atmospheric and monobloc gas burners.
