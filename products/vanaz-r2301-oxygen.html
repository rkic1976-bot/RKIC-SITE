Vanaz R2301 Oxygen Regulator

Product Code: R2301
TypeR2301
Connection25mm (1") BSPF Threaded End
GasOxygen
Flow Rate0 – 100 Nm³/Hr.
Inlet Pressure1 – 17 Kg/cm²
Outlet Pressure0.5 – 10 Kg/cm², spring-adjustable
Body MaterialAluminium Body & Bonnet, Brass Trim Parts
ManufacturerVanaz Engineers Ltd, Pune

The Vanaz R2301 is a spring-loaded pressure regulator built for oxygen gas service, reducing high-pressure cylinder or manifold supply down to a safe, stable working pressure for cutting, welding and industrial gas-line applications. Rated for a 1-17 kg/cm² inlet pressure down to a 0.5-10 kg/cm² adjustable outlet at flow rates up to 100 Nm3/hr, it is built with an aluminium body and bonnet with brass trim parts for oxygen-service compatibility. Outlet pressure is set via the top T-handle adjusting screw. Manufactured by Vanaz Engineers Ltd, Pune.
