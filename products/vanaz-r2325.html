Vanaz R2325 Medical Oxygen Pressure Regulator

Product Code: R2325
TypeR2325
Connection1" BSP, Internal / Female
GasMedical Oxygen (O₂)
Flow RateUp to 120 Nm³/hr
Inlet Pressure1 – 19 kg/cm²
Outlet Pressure0.5 – 8 kg/cm², spring-adjustable
Body MaterialForged brass / bronze body, oxygen-compatible
ManufacturerVanaz Engineers Ltd, Pune

The Vanaz R2325 is a spring-loaded pressure regulator built for medical oxygen service, reducing high-pressure manifold or cylinder supply down to a safe, stable working pressure for medical oxygen distribution lines and bulk industrial gas networks. Rated for 1-19 kg/cm2 inlet down to a 0.5-8 kg/cm2 adjustable outlet at flow rates up to 120 Nm3/hr, it is built with a forged brass/bronze body for oxygen-service compatibility, mounted in-line via threaded internal BSP ports. Outlet pressure is set via the top T-handle adjusting screw. Manufactured by Vanaz Engineers Ltd, Pune.
