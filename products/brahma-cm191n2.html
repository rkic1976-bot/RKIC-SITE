Brahma Sequence Controller CM 191N.2

Product Code: 20023101 · 230V, 50-60Hz, 7VA
TypeCM 191N.2
Product Code20083301
Pre-Purge Time (TW)1.5 s
Safety Time (TS)10 s
Voltage230V, 50-60Hz, 7VA
Min. Flame Signal1.2 µA
ComplianceCE 0051, EN298:2003 Compliant, RoHS Compliant

The Brahma CM 191N.2 is a burner sequence controller that manages the start-up, ignition, and flame-monitoring cycle of gas burners, with a 1.5-second pre-purge/wait time and a 10-second safety time. It monitors flame current directly with a minimum detectable flame signal of 1.2 µA, and features a front-panel reset/lockout indicator button for quick fault acknowledgement. Running on a 230V, 50-60Hz, 7VA supply, it is manufactured by Brahma, Italy, EN298:2003 compliant, for automatic burner control and flame safeguard duty on gas-fired burners and boilers.
