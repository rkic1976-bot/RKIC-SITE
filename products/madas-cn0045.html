Madas CN 0045 Solenoid Valve Coil Connector

Product Code: CN.0045 · 230 VAC, 1.5A
Product TypeDIN 43650 Rectifier-Type Coil Connector (AC-to-DC Valve Plug)
Part No.CN.0045
Supply Voltage230 VAC (also available for 110 VAC coils)
Current Rating1.5 A
FunctionBuilt-in bridge rectifier — converts the AC supply to rectified DC for MADAS solenoid valve coils that run on DC current
Connection StandardDIN 43650
IncludesConnector body, sealing gasket & fixing screw
ProtectionIP65 (with gasket fitted, per DIN 43650 standard)
Compatible WithMADAS gas solenoid valve coils wired for AC supply (e.g. DN15–DN25 valve range and others)
ManufacturerMADAS s.r.l., Italy
Country of OriginItaly

MADAS supplies the CN.0045 connector, a DIN 43650 coil plug fitted with a built-in bridge rectifier that converts a 230 VAC supply into rectified DC for MADAS gas solenoid valve coils designed to run on DC current — commonly used on the smaller-bore MADAS solenoid valve range (DN15–DN25) among others. Rated 230 VAC, 1.5 A, the connector kit is supplied complete with its sealing gasket and fixing screw for a secure, weatherproof DIN 43650 mount at the coil head. A standard MADAS gas-train accessory used wherever an AC-supplied MADAS DC-coil valve needs its rectifier/wiring connector replaced.
