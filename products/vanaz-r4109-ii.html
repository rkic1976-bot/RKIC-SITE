Pressure Regulator Valves R4109 II

Vanaz Low Pressure Regulator R4109 II

Product Code: R4109 II · 21.8x14 TPI (L.H.) or ½" BSP IN x ½" BSP OUT
TypeR4109 II
GasLPG, Nitrogen, Natural Gas, Air
Inlet Pressure1 – 17 bar
Outlet Pressure1.05 bar (15 PSI), spring-loaded & adjustable
Flow Rate0 – 28 Nm³/hr
Inlet Connection21.8 × 14 TPI (L.H.) Union Nut or ½" BSP (Internal)
Outlet Connection½" BSP (Internal) — common across R4109 range
ManufacturerVanaz Engineers Ltd., Pune

The Vanaz R4109 II is a spring-loaded low-pressure gas regulator with an adjustable outlet pressure around 1.05 bar (15 PSI), set by rotating the adjustment screw clockwise or anticlockwise to raise or lower the delivery pressure, from a 1–17 bar inlet, with a flow rate of 0–28 Nm³/hr. It handles LPG, Nitrogen, Natural Gas and Air, and is fitted with a 21.8 × 14 TPI (L.H.) union nut or ½" BSP (Internal) inlet and a ½" BSP (Internal) outlet — connections common across the R4109 model range. Manufactured by Vanaz Engineers Ltd., Pune, it delivers accurate low-pressure control for gas cylinder and appliance connections.
