Brahma Solenoid Valve E6G*SR10*1/2*GMO

Product Code: 13762002 · DN15 · 1/2" BSP
TypeE6G*SR10*1/2*GMO
ConnectionDN15 · 1/2" BSP
ClassClass A
Max PressureP max. 500 mbar
Power Supply230V, 50-60Hz, 20VA
ProtectionIP54
Solenoid TypeBE6*GMO · Code 18811001

The Brahma E6G*SR10*1/2*GMO is a Class A automatic gas solenoid valve for burner, boiler and gas-train shut-off duty on 1/2" (DN15) BSP connections. Rated to 500 mbar max inlet pressure with IP54 enclosure protection, it runs on a 230V, 50-60Hz, 20VA coil and pairs with the BE6*GMO solenoid coil for reliable, repeatable shut-off on industrial combustion lines.
