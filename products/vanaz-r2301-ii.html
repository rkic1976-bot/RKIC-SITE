Vanaz R2301 II Oxygen Gas Pressure Regulator

Product Code: R2301 II
TypeR2301 II
Connection25mm (1") BSPF Threaded End
GasOxygen / Nitrogen
Flow Rate0 – 100 Nm³/Hr.
Inlet Pressure15 – 120 Bar
Outlet Pressure1 – 10 Bar, spring-adjustable
Body MaterialBrass body, Aluminium bonnet, Brass trim parts
ManufacturerVanaz Engineers Ltd, Pune

The Vanaz R2301 II is a spring-loaded pressure regulator built for oxygen and nitrogen gas service, reducing high-pressure cylinder or manifold supply down to a safe, stable working pressure for cutting, welding and industrial gas-line applications. Rated for 15-120 bar inlet down to a 1-10 bar adjustable outlet at flow rates up to 100 Nm3/hr, it is built with a brass body and trim parts with an aluminium bonnet for oxygen-service compatibility. Outlet pressure is set via the top T-handle adjusting screw. Manufactured by Vanaz Engineers Ltd, Pune.
