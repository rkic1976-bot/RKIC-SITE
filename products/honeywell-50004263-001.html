Honeywell Modutrol Transformer 50004263-001

Product Code: 50004263-001 · Multi-tap primary, flying leads
Type50004263-001
PrimarySelectable: BLK-COM, BRN-24V, WHT-120V, BLU-230V, 50/60Hz
Secondary24VAC, 20VA (BRN-BRN)
RatingClass 2 (Not Wet), Class 3 (Wet)
InsulationISC Class 130 (B)
ApprovalsUL Listed (E126807)
ApplicationHoneywell Modutrol motor / burner control circuits
ManufacturerHoneywell

The Honeywell 50004263-001 is a multi-tap control transformer used to power Honeywell Modutrol motors and related burner/boiler control circuits. It accepts either 120V or 230V primary (50/60Hz) via selectable White/Blue taps against the Black common, and steps it down to a 24VAC, 20VA secondary (Brown-Brown) for driving low-voltage actuators and controls. Rated Class 2 for dry locations and Class 3 for wet locations, with Class 130(B) insulation system, it is UL listed. Manufactured by Honeywell, it is a common replacement part for Modutrol motor and burner control panel installations.
