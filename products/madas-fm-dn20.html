Madas FM DN 20 Gas Filter

Product Code: FM DN 20 · ¾" BSP
ModelFM DN 20
TypeGas Filter
Connection SizeDN 20 (¾" BSP)
Maximum Pressure (Pmax / PS)6 bar
Filtering Grade50 µm
CertificationCE-51AR1070, EN 126
CE Body / Notified Body0051 / 0497
Operating Temperature (TS)-40°C to +70°C
ManufacturerMadas

The Madas FM DN 20 is an in-line gas filter for gas control lines, sized DN 20 (¾" BSP connection), rated to a maximum working pressure (PS) of 6 bar with a filtering grade of 50 microns. It has an operating temperature range of -40°C to +70°C and is certified to EN 126 (CE-51AR1070). Manufactured by Madas.
