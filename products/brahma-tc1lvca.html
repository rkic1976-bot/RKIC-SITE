Brahma Ignition Transformer TC1LVCA

Product Code: 15911000 · Single-Point (1x15kV) Secondary Output
TypeTC1LVCA
Product Code15911000
Primary220–240V, 45VA, 50/60Hz
Secondary1 x 15kV Ampl. – 25mA
Duty CycleAB 50%, ED in 2 min., T60H
Output PointsSingle-Point (one HV output lead)
ComplianceRoHS Compliant, CE Marked

The Brahma TC1LVCA is a single-point ignition transformer that steps up a 220–240V mains supply to a single 15kV high-voltage secondary output rated at 25mA, for firing the spark electrode on gas and oil burners. Rated for an intermittent duty cycle of 50% ED (energised duration) within a 2-minute cycle (T60H), it is suited to burners with cyclic ignition demand. Manufactured by Brahma, Italy, it is RoHS compliant and CE marked for industrial burner and boiler ignition applications.
