Pressure Regulator Valves R2301

Vanaz R2301 High Pressure Regulator

Product Code: R2301 · 25mm · 1" BSP (F)
TypeR2301
Connection25mm · 1" BSPF Threaded End
GasLPG / NG / N2
Flow Rate0 – 100 Nm³/hr
Inlet Pressure1 – 17 kg/cm² (bar)
Outlet Pressure0.5 – 10 kg/cm² (bar), spring-adjustable
Body MaterialAluminium body & bonnet, brass trim parts
ManufacturerVanaz Engineers Ltd, Pune

The Vanaz R2301 is a first-stage, spring-loaded gas pressure regulator built for high-to-medium primary pressure reduction in gas manifolds, industrial burners, furnaces and gas train assemblies. It handles LPG, natural gas and nitrogen, and suits both horizontal and vertical pipeline layouts. Outlet pressure is set by rotating the top adjusting screw — clockwise to raise, counter-clockwise to lower — and the inline-serviceable design allows valve trim inspection without dismounting the body from the line. Manufactured by Vanaz Engineers Ltd, Pune.
