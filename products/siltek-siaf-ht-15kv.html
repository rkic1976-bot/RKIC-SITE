Ignition Cable SIAF/HT 15kV

Siltek SIAF/HT 15kV Silicone Ignition Cable

Product Code: SIAF/HT 15kV
Product TypeHigh Voltage (HT) Silicone Ignition Cable
ConductorBare / Tinned Stranded Copper (1 × 1 mm²)
Outer Diameter7 mm
Insulation MaterialHigh-grade, tear-resistant Silicone Rubber (Besilen / EI 2 grade)
Voltage Rating15 kV
Operating Temperature-50°C to +180°C (short-term up to +250°C)
Standard Packaging100-metre coil / skein (also available per metre)
ManufacturerSiltek

The Siltek SIAF/HT 15kV is a high-voltage silicone ignition cable used to connect ignition transformers to spark electrodes on gas and oil burners. Built with a bare/tinned stranded copper conductor (1x1 mm2) and a high-grade, tear-resistant silicone rubber insulation (Besilen / EI 2 grade), it has a 7mm outer diameter and is rated for 15 kV. It withstands a wide -50°C to +180°C continuous operating range, with a short-term rating up to +250°C, making it suitable for the high-heat environment near burner ignition assemblies. Supplied as a 100-metre coil/skein, also available per metre. Manufactured by Siltek.
