Pressure Regulator Valves R2316

Vanaz High Pressure Regulator R2316 1.5" Flgd #150

Product Code: R2316 · 40mm (1.5") Flanged End ANSI 150# Class
Inlet Pressure1 – 17 kg/cm² (bar)
Outlet Pressure0.5 – 10 kg/cm² (bar), spring-adjustable
Flow Rate0 – 250 Nm³/Hr
GasLPG / NG / N2
MOCSGCI Body, Aluminium Bonnet, Brass Trim Parts
Size40mm (1.5") Flanged End ANSI 150# Class
ManufacturerVanaz Engineers Ltd, Pune

The Vanaz R2316 is a flanged-end high-pressure regulator for LPG, natural gas and N2 service, stepping down inlet pressures of 1–17 kg/cm² (bar) to a controlled, spring-adjustable outlet of 0.5–10 kg/cm² (bar) at flows up to 250 Nm³/Hr. Unlike the threaded-end R2304 series, it connects via a 40mm (1.5") ANSI 150# class flanged end, making it suited for bolted gas train installations. Built with an SGCI body, aluminium bonnet and brass trim parts by Vanaz Engineers Ltd., Pune, it includes an inline filter that must be cleaned periodically to keep the regulator operating trouble-free.
