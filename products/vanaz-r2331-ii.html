Pressure Regulator Valves R2331 II

Vanaz R2331 II Medical Oxygen Regulator

Product Code: R2331 II · 1.5" BSP (Internal) End Connection
Inlet Pressure5 – 40 Kg/cm²
Outlet Pressure4 – 12 Kg/cm²
Flow Rate0 – 600 Nm³/Hr
GasMedical Oxygen
MOCCF8M (Stainless Steel) Body
Size1.5" BSP (Internal) End Connection
ManufacturerVanaz Engineers Ltd, Pune

The Vanaz R2331 II is a high-flow pressure regulator purpose-built for Medical Oxygen service, stepping down inlet pressures of 5–40 kg/cm² to a controlled outlet of 4–12 kg/cm² at flows up to 600 Nm³/Hr. Its wetted body is CF8M stainless steel, oxygen-service rated for cleanliness and corrosion resistance, with a 1.5" BSP internal-thread end connection for direct pipeline fitting. Manufactured by Vanaz Engineers Ltd., Pune, it is built for hospital, medical gas and industrial oxygen distribution systems where reliable, contamination-free pressure regulation is critical.
