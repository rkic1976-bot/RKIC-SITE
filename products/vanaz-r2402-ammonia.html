Pressure Regulator Valves R2402

Vanaz Ammonia Regulator R2402

Product Code: R2402 · 15mm (1/2″) BSPF
Inlet Pressure22 Bar
Outlet Pressure0.5 – 4 Bar, spring-adjustable
Flow Rate0 – 25 Nm³/Hr
GasAmmonia (NH3)
MOCAluminium Body & Bonnet, SS Trim Parts
Size15mm (1/2″) BSPF
ManufacturerVanaz Engineers Ltd, Pune

The Vanaz R2402 is a compact ammonia (NH3) pressure regulator rated for a high inlet pressure of 22 bar, with a spring-adjustable outlet pressure of 0.5 to 4 bar and a flow rate of up to 25 Nm³/Hr. Built specifically for ammonia gas service with an aluminium body and bonnet and stainless steel trim parts for corrosion resistance, it features a compact 15mm (1/2″) BSPF connection suited for smaller ammonia refrigeration and gas-train lines. Manufactured by Vanaz Engineers Ltd, Pune.
