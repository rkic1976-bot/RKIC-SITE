Elektrogas VMR2-5 Gas Solenoid Valve (Rp3/4)

Product Code: VMR2-5 · 230VAC, 50/60Hz, Rp3/4
ModelVMR2-5, Rp3/4
Supply Voltage230 VAC, 50/60 Hz
Power35 W
Max Pressure500 mbar
Ambient Temp.-15°C to +60°C
CertificationEN 161 Class A, Group 2, IP54
MarkingCE 0476

The Elektrogas VMR2-5 (Rp3/4) is a normally-closed automatic-reset gas solenoid valve for shut-off duty on gas lines, rated 230VAC 50/60Hz with a 35W coil and a maximum working pressure of 500 mbar. Certified to EN 161 Class A, Group 2, IP54, and CE marked (0476), it is rated for an ambient temperature range of -15°C to +60°C. Manufactured by Elektrogas for burner, boiler and gas-train safety shut-off applications on 3/4" (Rp3/4) BSP connections.
