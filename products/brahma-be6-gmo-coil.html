The Brahma BE6*GMO is a replacement solenoid coil designed for the EG12*SR*GMO series gas solenoid valve. Rated Class A with a maximum working pressure of 500 mbar, it runs on a 230V, 50-60Hz, 20VA supply with IP54 protection for reliable operation in industrial burner and boiler gas train installations. Manufactured by Brahma, Italy, it is RoHS compliant and CE marked, and is used as a spare coil to service or replace the coil on an existing EG12 valve without changing the valve body.
