Brahma E7/L*A3C Oil Solenoid Valve

Product Code: 13191801 · NC, P max. 20 bar

FunctionNormally Closed (NC)
Max Pressure20 bar
Coil230V, 50Hz, 11VA, IP65

Solenoid Coil Code18812026

The Brahma E7/L*A3C is a normally-closed (NC) solenoid valve rated for pressures up to 20 bar, designed for shut-off duty on light oil and diesel burner fuel lines. It operates on a 230V, 50Hz, 11VA coil with IP65 protection for reliable performance in industrial burner environments, and pairs with the BE7*A3C solenoid coil. Manufactured by Brahma, Italy and CE marked, it is built for oil burner and boiler fuel-line safety shut-off applications.
