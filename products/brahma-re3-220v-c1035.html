Brahma Flame Relay Sensor RE3 (220–230V) — Code 10801035

Product Code: 10801035 · 220–230V, 50–60Hz
TypeRE3
Product Code10801035
Supply Voltage220 – 230V
Frequency50 – 60Hz
Power3 VA
Safety Time (TS)max. 1 s
IndicatorsPower (green LED), Flame (red LED)

The Brahma RE3 (Code 10801035) is a flame relay module that takes the signal from a flame sensor (UV cell or photocell) and converts it into a switched relay output confirming flame presence, with a fast maximum safety time of 1 second. Front-panel Power and Flame LED indicators give an instant visual check of supply status and flame signal. This 220–230V, 50–60Hz, 3VA variant carries a distinct part number from the standard RE3 220–230V (Code 10801025) already in the catalog — always confirm the code on the label before ordering. Manufactured by Brahma, Italy for burner and boiler flame monitoring and safety circuits.
