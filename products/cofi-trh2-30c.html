Cofi Ignition Transformer TRH2-30C

The Cofi TRH2-30C is a two-point, earthed-centre ignition transformer that steps up a 230V, 50/60Hz primary supply (0.35A) to a dual 12kV secondary output (2 x 12kV) at 30mA RMS, for firing the spark electrodes on gas and oil burners requiring two ignition points from a single transformer. Rated for continuous 100% duty (ED 100%) at an ambient temperature of 60°C, IP00. Manufactured by Cofi srl, Italy, it is CE marked and suited to industrial burner and boiler ignition applications.
