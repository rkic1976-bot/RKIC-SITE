Danfoss EBI4 1P Ignition Transformer

ApplicationGas burner ignition (intermittent-cycle duty)
ComplianceCE Marking

The Danfoss EBI4 1P is a single-pole ignition transformer from the EBI4 series, built for intermittent-duty gas burner ignition rated 33% ED in 3 minutes. It steps up a 230V, 50-60Hz, 60VA primary supply to a single 12kV secondary output at 40mA (Ampl.), sparking between one electrode and the burner frame/earth. Replacing the earlier Danfoss EBI No. 052F0040, it is manufactured by Danfoss in Nordborg, Denmark, and is widely used on gas-fired burners (including Junior GAS-series units) that need a compact, cost-effective ignition source for intermittent-cycle operation.
