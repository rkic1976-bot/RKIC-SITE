Elektrogas VMR/L Solenoid Coil

Product Code: 1930.1313 · VMR/L series
TypeVMR/L 2/3 · 2-3 · 3-3 (360 mbar) / VMR/L 2-5 · 3-5 (500 mbar) / VMR/L 2-60 · 3-60 (6 bar)

ProtectionIP54
Operating Temp-15°C to +60°C
Gas TypeAir & non-aggressive gases (Family 1-2-3, EN 437)
ApprovalsCE EN 161, FM, EAC, Australian Gas Mark
OriginMade in Italy

The Elektrogas VMR/L is the coil/operator head for the VMR series of normally-closed, automatic-reset safety solenoid valves — fast-opening when energised and fast-closing the moment power is cut. It is built for gas and air blocking and control duty on atmospheric and fan-assisted burners, industrial kilns, and other continuous-service (100% ED) gas equipment, and is available across 360 mbar, 500 mbar and 6 bar pressure ratings to match the application. Manufactured by Elektrogas, Italy.
