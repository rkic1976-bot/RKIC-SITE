Pressure Regulator Valves R2304

Vanaz High Pressure Regulator R2304

Product Code: R2304 · 40mm (1.5") BSPF Threaded End
Inlet Pressure1 – 17 kg/cm² (bar)
Outlet Pressure0.5 – 10 kg/cm² (bar), spring-adjustable
Flow Rate0 – 250 Nm³/Hr
GasLPG / NG / N2
MOCSGCI Body, Aluminium Bonnet, Brass Trim Parts
Size40mm (1.5") BSPF Threaded End
ManufacturerVanaz Engineers Ltd, Pune

The Vanaz R2304 is a high-flow first-stage/primary pressure regulator for LPG, natural gas and N2 service, stepping down inlet pressures of 1–17 kg/cm² (bar) to a controlled, spring-adjustable outlet range of 0.5–10 kg/cm² (bar) at flows up to 250 Nm³/Hr. Built with an SGCI body, aluminium bonnet and brass trim parts, it features an easy top-mounted adjustment screw for precise setpoint control and 40mm (1.5") BSPF threaded end connections. Manufactured by Vanaz Engineers Ltd., Pune, it is suited for high-pressure gas train installations ahead of downstream regulation stages.
