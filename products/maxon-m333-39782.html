Honeywell Maxon M333 Spark Electrode

Product Code: 39782 · M333 (8.75 SI)
Model / DesignationM333 (8.75 SI)
Part No.39782
TypeDirect Spark Ignition Electrode
Length8.75 in
Connection1/2" NPT
ApplicationMaxon OvenPak burners — Model 400 OP2 sizes 445, 456, 470, 487, EB6, EB7, OPLE 70, OPLE EB100
ConstructionCeramic-insulated electrode rod with threaded mounting body
ManufacturerHoneywell Maxon

The Honeywell Maxon M333 (Part No. 39782, also designated 8.75 SI) is an 8.75-inch heavy-duty industrial spark ignitor providing direct-spark ignition for gas burners and thermal processing equipment. It features a 1/2" NPT mounting connection and is commonly used on Maxon OvenPak burners, including Model 400 OP2 sizes 445, 456, 470, 487, EB6, EB7, OPLE 70, and OPLE EB100. Manufactured by Honeywell Maxon.
