Brahma Sequence Controller SM 152N.2

Product Code: 24283961 · 230V, 50-60Hz, 7VA
TypeSM 152N.2
Product Code24283961
SeriesEurobox
Pre-Purge Time (TW)5 s
Safety Time (TS)10 s
Voltage230V, 50-60Hz, 7VA
ComplianceCE 0051, EN298:2003 Compliant, RoHS Compliant

The Brahma SM 152N.2 is a burner sequence controller from the Brahma "Eurobox" series, managing the start-up, ignition, and flame-monitoring cycle of gas burners with a 5-second pre-purge time and a 10-second safety time. Running on a 230V, 50-60Hz, 7VA supply, it is manufactured by Brahma, Italy, EN298:2003 compliant and RoHS compliant, for automatic burner control and flame safeguard duty on atmospheric and monobloc gas burners.
