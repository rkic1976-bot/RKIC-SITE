Elektrogas VMR4 Gas Solenoid Valve (Rp1½")

Product Code: VMR4 · 230VAC, 50/60Hz, Rp1½"
ModelVMR4, Rp1½"
Connection Size1½" (Rp1½)
Supply Voltage230 VAC, 50/60 Hz
Power20 VA (I max 0.5A)
Max Pressure360 mbar
Ambient Temp.-15°C to +60°C
CertificationEN 161 Class A, Group 2, IP54
MarkingCE 0694
OriginMade in Italy

The Elektrogas VMR4 (Rp1½") is a normally-closed automatic-reset gas solenoid valve for shut-off duty on gas lines, with a 1½" connection size, rated 230VAC 50/60Hz with a 20VA coil (0.5A max current) and a maximum working pressure of 360 mbar. Certified to EN 161 Class A, Group 2, IP54, and CE marked (0694), it is rated for an ambient temperature range of -15°C to +60°C. Manufactured by Elektrogas, Italy, for burner, boiler and gas-train safety shut-off applications on 1½" (Rp1½") BSP connections.
