Madas CN 2130 Solenoid Valve Coil Connector

Product Code: CN-2130 · 230 VAC, 50-60Hz
Product TypeDIN 43650 Rectifier-Type Coil Connector (Energy-Saving, Automatic-Reset AC-to-DC Valve Plug)
Part No.CN-2130
Supply Voltage230 VAC
Frequency50–60 Hz
FunctionBuilt-in bridge rectifier — delivers an energy-saving, automatic-reset rectified DC holding current to the valve coil once energised
Connection StandardDIN 43650
ProtectionIP65 (with gasket fitted, per DIN 43650 standard)
Compatible WithMADAS EVP / EVPC series normally-closed (NC) gas solenoid valves, DN15–DN20
ManufacturerMADAS s.r.l., Italy
Country of OriginItaly

MADAS supplies the CN-2130 connector, a DIN 43650 coil plug fitted with a built-in bridge rectifier that provides an energy-saving, automatic-reset rectified DC holding current for MADAS gas solenoid valve coils — commonly used on the EVP/EVPC series normally-closed (NC) gas solenoid valve range (DN15–DN20). Rated 230 VAC, 50–60 Hz, it mounts directly onto the coil head in the standard weatherproof DIN 43650 form. A standard MADAS gas-train accessory used wherever an EVP/EVPC NC valve needs its energy-saving rectifier/wiring connector replaced.
