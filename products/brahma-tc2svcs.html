Brahma Ignition Transformer TC2SVCS

Product Code: 15911350 · Two-Point (2x12kV) Secondary Output
TypeTC2SVCS
Product Code15911350
Primary220–240V, 30VA, 50/60Hz
Secondary2 x 12kV Ampl. – 20mA
Duty CycleAB 100%, T60H
Output PointsTwo-Point (dual HV output leads)
ComplianceRoHS Compliant, CE Marked

The Brahma TC2SVCS is a two-point ignition transformer that steps up a 220–240V mains supply to two independent 12kV high-voltage secondary outputs, each rated at 20mA, for firing spark electrodes on gas and oil burners. Rated for continuous 100% duty (T60H), it supplies both ignition electrodes from a single compact unit via its dual HV output leads, simplifying wiring on twin-electrode burner ignition systems. Manufactured by Brahma, Italy, it is RoHS compliant and CE marked for industrial burner and boiler ignition applications.
