Pressure Regulator Valves R2304 IV

Vanaz High Pressure Regulator R2304 IV

Product Code: R2304 IV · 50mm (2") BSPF Threaded End
Inlet Pressure1 – 17 Bar
Outlet Pressure0.5 – 10 Bar, spring-adjustable
Flow Rate0 – 250 Nm³/Hr
GasLPG / NG / N2
MOCSGCI Body, Aluminium Bonnet, Brass Trim Parts
Size50mm (2") BSPF Threaded End
ManufacturerVanaz Engineers Ltd, Pune

The Vanaz R2304 IV is a high pressure regulator rated for an inlet pressure of 1 to 17 bar, with a spring-adjustable outlet pressure of 0.5 to 10 bar and a flow rate of up to 250 Nm³/Hr, suitable for LPG, natural gas, and N2 service. It features an SGCI body with an aluminium bonnet and brass trim parts, and a 50mm (2") BSPF threaded end connection. Manufactured by Vanaz Engineers Ltd, Pune.
