Angled (Bent-Tip) Special Ignition Electrode — Threaded Spark-Plug Type Igniter

Product Code: Custom / Made to Order · M14x1.25 / M18x1.50 Thread · Angled (ZK Type)
Product TypeAngled (Bent-Tip) Special Ignition Electrode (Threaded Spark-Plug Type Igniter, 90° Angled Ground Arm)
Thread / Mounting SizeM14 x 1.25 or M18 x 1.50 — per customer specification
Overall Length (LG)Customer specified — typical range 35 mm to 600 mm
Electrode Configuration90° angled ground / mass arm — approx. 4.0 mm spark gap
Center Electrode MaterialChromium-Silicon Stainless Steel — other materials on request
Ground / Mass Arm MaterialNickel-Chromium Alloy (NiCr) — other materials on request
Electrode & Wiring MaterialKanthal A1 (FeCrAl resistance wire)
Insulator MaterialLow-loss, alkali-free Steatite Ceramic or 92–95% Aluminium Oxide
Typical ApplicationGas engines, CHP / cogeneration plants, biogas & natural gas gensets, industrial burners
CustomizationBend angle, thread size, length and material available on demand
ManufacturerR.K. Instruments & Controls

R.K. Instruments & Controls manufactures and supplies Angled (Bent-Tip) Special Ignition Electrodes — threaded, spark-plug-style igniters with a 90° angled ground/mass arm, used for direct spark ignition in gas engines, CHP (Combined Heat & Power) plants, biogas and natural gas generator sets, and industrial burners. Built to the widely used M14 x 1.25 or M18 x 1.50 threaded mounting format, each unit is manufactured to order with a chromium-silicon stainless-steel center electrode, nickel-chromium ground arm, and low-loss steatite or high-alumina ceramic insulation. Bend angle, overall length, thread size and materials can all be customised on request for gas engine OEMs, CHP operators and burner service applications.
