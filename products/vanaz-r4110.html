Pressure Regulator Valves R4110

Vanaz Low Pressure Regulator R4110

Product Code: R4110 · ½" BSP Inlet x ¾" BSP Outlet
TypeR4110
Inlet Pressure1 – 17 kg/cm²
Outlet Pressure0.03 – 0.3 kg/cm² (30 – 300 mbar), spring-adjustable
Flow Rate30 Nm³/hr
End Connection½" BSP (Internal) Inlet x ¾" BSP (Internal) Outlet, Threaded
MediaAir, LPG, Natural Gas, Carbon Dioxide, Nitrogen, Hydrogen, Acetylene
Body MaterialCast aluminium body, adjustable outlet via spring screw
ManufacturerVanaz Engineers Ltd, Pune

The Vanaz R4110 is a low-pressure, medium-flow gas pressure regulator designed for small industrial boilers, furnaces and commercial appliances such as canteen and restaurant kitchens. It steps down a 1–17 kg/cm² inlet pressure to a controlled, spring-adjustable 0.03–0.3 kg/cm² (30–300 mbar) outlet at up to 30 Nm³/hr, with a ½" BSP threaded inlet and ¾" BSP threaded outlet. It handles air, LPG, natural gas, carbon dioxide, nitrogen, hydrogen and acetylene, and is manufactured by Vanaz Engineers Ltd, Pune, for use across small-to-medium gas-fired equipment installations in India.
