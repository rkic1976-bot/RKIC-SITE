The Honeywell Elster Quantometer QA16 25 G I is a turbine-type gas flow meter for industrial, non-fiscal metering, sized DN25 (1" FNPT) with a flow range of 2 to 25 m³/h (Qmin to Qmax). A free-spinning turbine wheel is set in motion by the gas flow, with the number of revolutions proportional to the volume passed, read out via a mechanical roller counter in m³. Rated to a maximum operating pressure of 4 bar, it covers an operating temperature range of -10°C to +60°C, and is ATEX/IECEx certified for use in hazardous areas — hazardous area rating II 2G Ex h IIC T4 Gb, IECEx TUR 16.0043X, CE 0085. Commonly used for in-plant allocation and consumption monitoring on boilers, furnaces and heating systems running on natural gas and other non-corrosive gases. Manufactured by Elster Instromet (Honeywell Elster).
