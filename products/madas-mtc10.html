Valve Proving Systems MTC10

Madas MTC10 Valve Proving System

Product Code: MTC10 · 110V/230V AC · IP65
TypeMTC10 Valve Proving System (VPS)
Part No.MTC10
FunctionAutomatic valve-proving (leak-tightness) control unit — tests the shut-off valves for leaks before every gas burner start
Supply Voltage110 VAC or 230 VAC (order-specific)
Power Consumption3.2 VA
Output RatingsWorking output 2A · Fault output 1A · Fuse 6.3A (delayed)
Test Cycle DurationApprox. 60 seconds
Operation ModeContinuous automatic testing on every start
Valve CompatibilityMADAS EVP / CB-CK solenoid valves, DN15–DN100 (1 or 2 valves)
Pressure Switch Inputs1 or 2 pressure switches, per EN 1854
Operating Temperature-20°C to +60°C
ProtectionIP65
InstallationAny mounting position
StandardsEN 1643 (Valve Proving Systems) · EN 161 (Automatic Shut-off Valves) · EN 1854 (Pressure Sensing Devices)
ManufacturerMADAS s.r.l., Italy
Country of OriginItaly

The MADAS MTC10 is an automatic valve-proving (leak-tightness) control unit that checks the shut-off valves of a gas train for leaks before every burner start, per EN 1643. It monitors 1 or 2 MADAS solenoid valves (EVP / CB-CK series, DN15–DN100) together with 1 or 2 pressure switches (EN 1854), running a roughly 60-second test cycle on each start; front-panel FAULT/OK LEDs (with separate V1/V2 valve-status and P/pressure indicators) show the result, and a RESET button clears a fault lockout. Rated 110V or 230V AC, 3.2 VA, with a 2A working output and 1A fault output (6.3A delayed fuse), IP65 enclosure rated -20°C to +60°C for any mounting position. A standard MADAS gas-train safety accessory for industrial burner and heating plants, used wherever automatic valve-proving is mandated ahead of the solenoid valves.
