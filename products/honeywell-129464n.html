Honeywell UV Flame Detector Bulb 129464N

Product Code: 129464N · Multi-pin base, plug-in
Type129464N
ApplicationUV Flame Detector Bulb / Scanner Tube
MountingMulti-pin base, plug-in to scanner housing
Operating Temp.-40°F and above
ManufacturerHoneywell

The Honeywell 129464N is a UV-sensitive detector bulb (UV tube) used inside Honeywell UV flame scanners and flame safeguard amplifiers to sense the ultraviolet radiation emitted by gas or oil flames. It plugs into the scanner housing via its multi-pin base and is rated for operation down to -40°F, making it suitable for a wide range of industrial burner and boiler installations. Manufactured by Honeywell, it is a common replacement bulb for UV flame detection amplifier assemblies used in burner management and flame safeguard systems.
