Brahma Ignition Transformer TC1SVCS

Product Code: 15911250 · Single-Point (1x15kV) Secondary Output
TypeTC1SVCS
Product Code15911250
Primary220–240V, 25VA, 50/60Hz
Secondary1 x 15kV Ampl. – 15mA
Duty CycleAB 100%, T60H
Output PointsSingle-Point (one HV output lead)
ComplianceRoHS Compliant, CE Marked

The Brahma TC1SVCS is a single-point ignition transformer that steps up a 220–240V mains supply to a single 15kV high-voltage secondary output rated at 15mA, for firing the spark electrode on gas and oil burners. Rated for continuous 100% duty (T60H), it delivers a compact, reliable HV source for single-electrode burner ignition systems, with flexible HV lead routing on the transformer body. Manufactured by Brahma, Italy, it is RoHS compliant and CE marked for industrial burner and boiler ignition applications.
