Brahma EG40*L*GMO Gas Solenoid Valve

The Brahma EG40*L*GMO is a gradual-opening (slow-start) Class A gas solenoid valve rated for a maximum pressure of 200 mbar, opening progressively over 120 seconds on a 230V, 50-60Hz, 37W supply with a 138W start draw. Built with IP54 protection for reliable operation in industrial burner and boiler gas train installations, it runs on the BE9*GMO solenoid coil. Manufactured by Brahma, Italy, it is RoHS compliant and CE marked, and is suited to burner applications needing a smooth, gradual gas ramp-up rather than the fast-opening (SR-type) EG40 variant.
