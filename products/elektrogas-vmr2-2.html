The Elektrogas VMR2-2 is a solenoid coil sized Rp3/4, rated 230 VAC, 50/60 Hz, 25 VA, for use on natural gas, LPG (gaseous), and air service. It is rated to a maximum pressure of 200 mbar, with an IP54 protection rating and an operating temperature range of -15°C to +60°C (ambient equal to fluid temperature). Certified to EN 161, Class A - Group 2, CE marked (0476-22). Manufactured by Elektrogas, Italy.
