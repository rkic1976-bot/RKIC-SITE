Honeywell Elster BK-G16M Diaphragm Gas Meter G16

The Honeywell Elster BK-G16M is a G16-size diaphragm gas meter rated for a minimum flow of 0.16 m³/h, transitional flow of 4.0 m³/h and maximum flow of 25 m³/h, with a cyclic volume of 6.0 litres. It is built as a two-pipe diaphragm meter in a pressed steel casing with a pale grey (RAL 7035) powder-coated finish, and reads out via a mechanical roller counter (Vtb, in m³, with a red sub-decimal digit). Rated to a maximum operating pressure of 0.5 bar with a pressure loss of ≤ 0.1 bar at Qmax, it covers an operating gas temperature range of -25°C to +40°C and an ambient/measuring temperature range of -25°C to +55°C. Suitable for natural gas, PNG, LPG, propane, butane, town gas, air, and other inert non-corrosive gases, it can be retrofitted with a low-frequency pulse transmitter (e.g. IN-Z61) for remote reading, and is EN 1359:1998/A1:2006 compliant, MID approved and CE/DVGW certified. Manufactured by Honeywell Elster.
