Kromschröder Ignition Transformer TZI 5-15/100WE

Product Code: 84391150 · TZI 5-15/100WE
TypeTZI 5-15/100WE
Primary230V, 50(60)Hz, 0.4(0.3)A
Secondary1x5kV, 15(10)mA, Ampl. 5kV
Duty CycleED 100%, ta 35°C/B
ProtectionIP00
ManufacturerElster GmbH, Osnabrück

The Kromschröder TZI 5-15/100WE is a high-voltage ignition transformer that steps up standard 230V mains supply to a 5kV secondary output for firing the spark electrode on gas and oil burners. Rated for continuous 100% duty cycle operation, it is built by Elster GmbH (Osnabrück) under the Kromschröder brand, now part of Honeywell, for reliable ignition in industrial and commercial burner and boiler installations.
