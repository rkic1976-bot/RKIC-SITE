Brahma Photocell F8

Product Code: 18210025 · RAR9-type plug-in socket
TypeF8
Product Code18210025
Sensing ElementCadmium-Sulphide (CdS) Photoresistive Cell
ApplicationOil burner flame detection
MountingPlug-in, RAR9-type control box socket
Body ColourRed housing, black connector base
ManufacturerBrahma, Italy

The Brahma F8 (Part No. 18210025) is a photoresistive cell (photocell) used for flame detection on oil burners, plugging directly into the standard RAR9-type socket on the burner control box in place of the ionization probe circuit. Its cadmium-sulphide sensing element responds to the visible light emitted by an oil flame, signalling the burner control to confirm flame presence and continue the firing sequence, or to lock out if no flame is detected. Manufactured by Brahma, Italy, it is part of the FC7/F8 photocell family and is a common replacement part for oil-fired burner and boiler flame safeguard circuits.
