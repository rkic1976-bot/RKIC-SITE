Pressure Regulator Valves R2301 I

Vanaz Ammonia Regulator R2301 I

Product Code: R2301 I · 25mm (1") BSPF Threaded End
Inlet Pressure1 – 17 Bar
Outlet Pressure0.5 – 4 Bar, spring-adjustable
Flow Rate0 – 100 Nm³/Hr
GasAmmonia (NH3)
MOCAluminium Body & Bonnet, SS Trim Parts
Size25mm (1") BSPF Threaded End
ManufacturerVanaz Engineers Ltd, Pune

The Vanaz R2301 I is an ammonia (NH3) pressure regulator rated for an inlet pressure of 1 to 17 bar, with a spring-adjustable outlet pressure of 0.5 to 4 bar and a flow rate of up to 100 Nm³/Hr. Built specifically for ammonia gas service with an aluminium body and bonnet and stainless steel trim parts for corrosion resistance, it features a 25mm (1") BSPF threaded end connection. Manufactured by Vanaz Engineers Ltd, Pune.
