Who We Are

Since 1999, sourcing the right part, the first time.

Founded in 1999 out of Surat, Gujarat, R.K. Instruments & Controls is a trusted manufacturer, exporter, importer and supplier of burner, boiler and gas-handling components, working directly with dealers and distributors across India to get the right part to oil, gas, chemical, pharmaceutical and thermal plants every day.

As a stockist for ignition electrodes to full valve-proving systems, drawn from established global manufacturers, we make sure an engineer can specify a part once and trust it will still be in stock — and still be correct — years later.
